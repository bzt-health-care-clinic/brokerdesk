Excluded from the source copy:

- local environment files and secrets (.env.local)
- databases and database sidecars
- generated output, caches, dependencies, and build artifacts
- PDFs, office documents, archives, raster images, and logs

- personal names
- email addresses and phone numbers
- street and risk addresses
- VINs and driver-licence numbers
- policy, claim, quote, and membership-style identifiers
